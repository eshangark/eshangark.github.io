About me

I was born and raised near Chicago, and later lived in Houston and Pune, India, where I went to boarding school. I live in Las Vegas now, with most of my year spent away at school.

I’m studying Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Virginia. I’m especially interested in probability, statistics, finance, and computing, while also beginning to explore ethics and public policy.

I’m currently building Parallax NBA ↗, a mathematical model that ranks NBA teams and estimates win probabilities, with a dashboard for exploring matchups and making more informed betting decisions. Originally a personal betting tool, it’s now open source and available online.

Outside of school, I follow basketball, play chess, keep up with markets and investing, and work at the bookstore. If you want to talk about any of it, feel free to reach out.
